Smoke'Em privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Smoke'Em is a VR driving game made by Countersteer Studio, "we" below. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how you control it. Short version: play stats are off until you say yes, they never include your name or voice, and you can turn them off and have them deleted at any time.

1. Play stats (optional, off until you agree)

The first time you open the game it asks: "Help improve Smoke'Em by sharing anonymous play stats?" Nothing is collected or sent before you answer, and nothing is sent if you answer no. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy → Share play stats.

If you say yes, the game sends:

  • Sessions: when you start and stop playing, how many minutes you played, the game version, your headset model, whether you play in VR or on a flat screen, and your hand-tracking setting.
  • Rounds: for each finished round or race: the game mode, the track, the number of players, spectators and AI cars, the AI level, how long the round lasted, your final place, the coins you earned, and whether you played offline, hosted or joined (and as a driver or a passenger).
  • Menu choices that shape the game: game mode, track, number and level of AI cars, assist preset, pedal and gun-hand settings, hand tracking, crew and friendly-fire settings.
  • Midnight Run: how many hours of the night a run cleared, whether it reached dawn, crew size and which upgrades were taken.
  • Cosmetics: which cosmetic item (gloves, paint, smoke colour...) you equip or buy with in-game coins, and its price in coins. There is no real-money purchase in the game.
  • Unity's standard data that comes with the analytics service: device and operating system details (model, OS version, memory, screen), the app's start and end, and a country estimated by Unity from your connection. We do not receive your IP address.

All of this is tied to a random ID created on your headset (and, if you play online, the anonymous player ID described in section 2). It is not linked to your Meta account, your name, your email or your in-game name.

Why: to learn which modes, tracks and settings people actually play, where the game is too hard or too easy, and what to build next. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising or share it with anyone except the processor below.

Who processes it: Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services Analytics) stores and processes the data on our behalf as our data processor. See Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

How long: raw events are kept for the retention period of Unity Analytics (set by Unity, see their documentation) and are then deleted; we only keep aggregated numbers (for example "rounds played per mode per day") after that.

1b. High scores with friends (only with play stats ON)

When play stats are ON, the game also keeps friends' high scores on Unity Leaderboards: at the end of a finished Gymkhana run, a Drift Battle round you drove, or a Midnight Run, it sends your score and your in-game name (letters and digits only) with your anonymous Unity player ID. To show you how you compare, it reads the best scores of the players you have been in an online game with (their IDs are remembered on your headset only, up to 200) or, if there are none yet, the top 3 scores. Your name and best scores can be seen by other players of the game. With play stats OFF nothing is sent or read. Processed by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services Leaderboards) as our processor.

2. Online play and voice chat (only when you play online)

  • Online games run through Unity's relay and session services. To connect you, the game signs in to Unity Authentication anonymously: it creates a random player ID, with no account, email or password. Your in-game name (which you choose, default "PLAYER") is shown only to the players in your game.
  • Voice chat uses Unity Vivox. Your voice is streamed live to the other players in your game while your microphone is on. We do not record, store or listen to voice chat. You can turn the microphone off in Settings → Driving → Microphone.
  • Meta Quest features (Meta Horizon Platform). The game uses your Meta account to:
    • check that you own the game (entitlement check);
    • read your Meta user ID and username: the username becomes your in-game name if you never chose one (you can change it at any time); the ID is used only on your headset to talk to Meta's services and is not sent to us or to other players;
    • show your online game as joinable to your Meta friends while you host or play online (group presence: the game's 6-character join code, nothing else), and open Meta's invite panel when you choose INVITE FRIENDS, so you can invite friends and they can join you from the invitation (deep linking). This data is handled by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy; we do not store it.

3. Data that stays on your headset

Your settings, your in-game name, your coin balance and the list of players you have played online with are saved only on your headset (they are not sent to us, except your name with your high scores when play stats are ON: see 1b). Uninstalling the game removes them.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Turn play stats off: Settings → Privacy → Share play stats → OFF. The game stops collecting at once and asks Unity to delete the play stats already sent from your headset.
  • Questions, access or deletion requests: email us at smokeem-vr@proton.me (or open an issue at https://github.com/MayeulHP/smokeem-legal/issues). If you are in the EU / UK you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • Play stats are based on your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)); online play, voice and the Meta Quest features need the data in section 2 to work at all (art. 6(1)(b)).

5. Children

Smoke'Em is meant for players aged 13 and over (it contains cartoon violence and in-game smoking). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has shared play stats, email smokeem-vr@proton.me and we will have them deleted.

6. Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and, for anything new in play stats, ask again in the game.
